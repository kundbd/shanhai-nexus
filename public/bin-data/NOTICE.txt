The index keeps only six-digit prefixes and their reported issuing region.
Rows without a region and disagreements between sources are omitted unless
the dataset has a specific correction. It is a public reference snapshot.

To rebuild, extract bins.csv and conflicts.csv from the pinned source and run
python scripts/build-bin-regions.py SOURCE_DIR public/bin-data/issuer-regions-fcc907c.bin

The supplement keeps exact published six- and eight-digit prefixes. The bcbc
import selects reviewed mainland issuers and omits conflicts with overseas or
unreviewed records. Public issuer and partner disclosures take precedence over
the older region snapshot for the published prefix. An exact eight-digit match
takes priority over a six-digit match. New or unlisted card ranges may be absent.

To rebuild, extract bin.csv and name.csv from the pinned bcbc source and run
node scripts/build-bin-mainland.mjs SOURCE_DIR
