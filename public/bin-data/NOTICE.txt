Shanhai Nexus BIN region index

Source: Techbuddie Solutions, binlist-data (2026-09-03 snapshot, fcc907c).
https://github.com/Techbuddie-Solutions/binlist-data/tree/fcc907c81cf02b12e396b0c12a8c16472b47619a

Upstream sources:
Ian Nuttall, binlist-data — https://github.com/iannuttall/binlist-data
Venelin Kochev, bin-list-data — https://github.com/venelinkochev/bin-list-data

The source and this adapted index are available under CC BY 4.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The index keeps only six-digit prefixes and their reported issuing region.
Rows without a region and disagreements between sources are omitted unless
the dataset has a specific correction. It is a public reference snapshot.

To rebuild, extract bins.csv and conflicts.csv from the pinned source and run
python scripts/build-bin-regions.py SOURCE_DIR public/bin-data/issuer-regions-fcc907c.bin

Mainland China supplement

bcbc by Runrioter, snapshot de631827ffe8db2792d140f3476b02498fc1244f (MIT).
https://github.com/hexindai/bcbc/tree/de631827ffe8db2792d140f3476b02498fc1244f
License: LICENSE-bcbc.txt in this directory.

Additional public records:
ICBC card product: https://m.icbc.com.cn/page/721854222751383576.html
Shenzhen Human Resources and Social Security Bureau, social security card BINs:
https://szeb.sz.gov.cn/attachment/1/1447/1447926/11303034.pdf
Jilin University, payroll card prefixes: https://talent.jlu.edu.cn/info/1036/1372.htm

The supplement keeps exact published six- and eight-digit prefixes. The bcbc
import selects reviewed mainland issuers and omits conflicts with overseas or
unreviewed records. A verified eight-digit match takes priority over a six-digit
match. New or otherwise unlisted card ranges may be absent.

To rebuild, extract bin.csv from the pinned bcbc source and run
node scripts/build-bin-mainland.mjs SOURCE_DIR
